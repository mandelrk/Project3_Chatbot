Short answer: Generally you must notify your credit card issuer within 60 days of the date you received the first monthly statement that listed the charge for the transportation.

What to do and what to include
- Send your dispute to the address on your monthly statement that is for disputing charges (this may be different from the payment address).  
- Include: your account number; a photocopy of the ticket/itinerary/receipt; if you used part of the trip, identify used and unused segments; and a statement that the airline is in bankruptcy/ceased operations, you did not receive the service, and you are requesting a credit pursuant to the Fair Credit Billing Act.

Notes and tips
- File as soon as possible — the 60‑day window is firm in most cases.  
- Check your cardholder agreement and contact your issuer (and travel insurance provider, if applicable) for any additional steps or deadlines.  
- You can also consider filing a proof of claim in the airline’s bankruptcy case, but recoveries there are often limited and slow.
