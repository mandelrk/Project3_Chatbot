Short answer: No — name elements on an Air India PNR cannot be changed by a travel agent once the PNR is created.

Details and what you should do:
- Agents must not perform name modifications or cancel-and-replace names in the PNR via GDS or the airline website.  
- For simple misspellings, contact Air India Reservation offices (they may consider corrections case‑by‑case). Corrections are limited (maximum of three characters changed) and must be supported with documentation.  
- Any name correction should be requested at least 72 hours before departure.  
- If a ticket has already been issued and a correction is needed, Air India’s process requires creating a new PNR with the correct name, issuing a new ticket (matching itinerary), cross‑referencing PNRs in endorsements, and following airline helpdesk guidance if the original RBD is unavailable. Refund of the incorrect ticket may also be required.  
- Note: For bookings issued from the UK, Europe or Israel there may be a nominal name‑change fee (POS dependent).
