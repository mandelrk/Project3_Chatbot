Air India’s waiver says a domestic-only flight (not connected to an international itinerary) must be rescheduled by more than 1 hour to qualify for the waiver that allows a full refund.

If you need to process the refund, follow the policy steps:
- Cancellation charge is waived.  
- Add the OSI/coupon remark for the cancelled flight: e.g. "OSI: AI CANCEL / AI ### / DDMMYY" (replace ### and date).  
- Ensure the entire booking/PNR is cancelled to avail full refund.  
- Agent can then process the refund through the GDS.
