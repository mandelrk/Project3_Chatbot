Churning is when an agent makes repeated bookings for the same passenger on the same sector (or overlapping sectors) — either within a single PNR or across multiple PNRs, on the same GDS or across different GDSs. Air India permits up to 4 churns for Economy-class bookings and up to 10 for Premium-class bookings; after that an ADM of US$10 is charged per additional churn per passenger per segment. Churn is judged using parameters such as same origin, destination, passenger name and airline IATA code.
