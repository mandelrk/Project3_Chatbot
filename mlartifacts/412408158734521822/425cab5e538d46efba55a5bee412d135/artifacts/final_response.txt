Booking tips
- Pick your hotel based on neighborhood and commute time (Mumbai traffic can add lots of travel time).  
- Book earlier for Nov–Feb (peak season) and avoid the heavy-monsoon months (Jun–Sep) if you want clear skies.  
- Compare rates and check cancellation and rebooking policies. Ask about airport pickup, breakfast inclusion and city/local taxes.  
- For group or corporate bookings, request negotiated rates and flexible cancellation.  
- If paying by card, check the property’s accepted cards and any surcharges — policies vary by hotel and country.
